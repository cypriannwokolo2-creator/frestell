Services — Extractable Microservices

Each service is independently deployable with its own package.json, Dockerfile, and src/ structure. All follow NestJS pattern.

Notes:

  • Keep published interfaces small and well-documented.
  • CI: build and test each service independently; publish Docker images with semantic tags.
  • At scale, map each service to its own host/container orchestration unit (ECS, Kubernetes, Cloud Run).
